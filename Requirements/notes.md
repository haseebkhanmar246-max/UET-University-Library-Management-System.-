Software Requirements Specification (SRS): A formal, baseline document capturing the complete functional and non-functional boundaries of the university library system.

User Personas & Stakeholders: Detailed profiles covering students, faculty members, librarians, and system administrators to guide system workflows.

Core Functional Specifications: Documented rules for user authentication, catalog searches, circulation processing, and reservation handling.

Use Case Descriptions: Step-by-step narrative descriptions mapping primary actor interactions to system responses for every major transaction.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix: Clear definition of permissions separating student viewing rights from librarian management capabilities.

Inventory Management Rules: Specifications for indexing physical and digital items by ISBN, author, title, and category.

Circulation Policy Guidelines: Standardized rules for loan durations, renewal limits, and automated fine calculations ($0.50/day).

Non-Functional Quality Attributes: Performance benchmarks ensuring sub-2-second search response times and 99.5% system availability
